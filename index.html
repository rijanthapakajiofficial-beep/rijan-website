About Me

A passionate soul
from the mountains

Hey there! I'm Rijan Thapa Kaji — a music-loving, adventure-chasing creative from the beautiful land of Nepal. 🇳🇵

I carry the pride of the Thapa Kaji heritage — a lineage of strength, honor, and deep roots in Nepali culture. My grandfather Tirtha Bahadur Thapa planted the seeds of this family tree in 2022 BS, and every generation since has blossomed from that proud foundation.

Whether I'm strumming a melody that echoes through Himalayan valleys, hiking hidden trails above the clouds, or capturing fleeting golden-hour moments through a lens — I pour my whole heart into it. Music is my language, the mountains are my home, and stories are what I leave behind.

Born in 2064 BS into a family that taught me to love deeply and live fully, I'm on a journey to create, explore, and connect. Scroll down to meet the people who made me who I am. 🙏
