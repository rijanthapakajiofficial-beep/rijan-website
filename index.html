About Me

A passionate soul
from the mountains

Hey there! I'm Rijan Thapa Kaji — a music-loving, adventure-chasing creative from the beautiful land of Nepal. 🇳🇵

I carry the pride of the Thapa Kaji heritage — a lineage of strength, honor, and deep roots in Nepali culture.

Whether I'm strumming a melody, exploring hidden trails in the Himalayas, or capturing life through a lens, I pour my heart into everything I do.

Follow along on my journey, connect with me, or just say hello. Let's make something memorable together. 🙏
